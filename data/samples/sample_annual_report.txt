ACME CORPORATION
Annual Report 2023

LETTER TO SHAREHOLDERS

Dear Fellow Shareholders,

Fiscal year 2023 was a year of strong execution and strategic progress for ACME Corporation.
We delivered revenue of $4.2 billion, representing 12% year-over-year growth, driven by
continued momentum in our cloud software division and disciplined cost management.

Net income attributable to common shareholders was $612 million, or $3.84 per diluted share,
compared with $489 million, or $3.07 per diluted share, in fiscal 2022. Adjusted EBITDA
margin expanded 180 basis points to 28.4%.

We returned $320 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during
the year, reflecting our confidence in the durability of our cash flows.

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Cloud Software Division
Revenue grew 24% to $2,100M, fueled by net new customer additions of 1,400 and
expansion within our existing customer base. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached
$1,950M, up from $1,560M in the prior year. Net revenue retention rate was 118%,
indicating strong upsell and cross-sell performance.

Industrial Products Division
Revenue of $1,680M was flat compared to the prior year, reflecting softness in
European markets partially offset by strength in Asia-Pacific. Operating margin
contracted 90 basis points to 15.2% due to elevated raw material costs.

Financial Services Division
Revenue increased 8% to $420M. The division launched two new financial analytics
products and expanded its enterprise client roster to 340 institutions globally.

RISK FACTORS

The Company's business is subject to a number of material risks, including:

1. Macroeconomic Risk: A deterioration in global economic conditions could reduce
   customer spending on software and technology products.

2. Competition: The cloud software market is highly competitive. Competitors include
   large, well-resourced technology companies as well as emerging startups.

3. Cybersecurity: The Company processes sensitive financial data and is a potential
   target for cyberattacks. A successful breach could result in reputational and
   financial harm.

4. Regulatory: Changes in data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) or financial
   services regulations could increase compliance costs or restrict product capabilities.

5. Foreign Exchange: Approximately 38% of revenue is denominated in currencies other
   than the U.S. dollar. Currency fluctuations can materially affect reported results.

OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL 2024

Management expects:
- Total revenue in the range of $4,600M to $4,700M (approximately 10-12% growth)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin of 29.0% to 29.5%
- Free cash flow of approximately $950M to $1,000M
- Capital expenditures of approximately $180M

The Board of Directors has approved a quarterly dividend of $0.32 per share,
representing a 7% increase from the prior year.

ABOUT ACME CORPORATION

ACME Corporation (NASDAQ: ACME) is a global provider of cloud software, industrial
products, and financial analytics services. Founded in 1985 and headquartered in
San Francisco, California, the Company employs approximately 18,500 people in 32 countries.
